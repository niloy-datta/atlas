## Completed Phases

- **Phase 0–6**: Platform foundation, identity boundary, private/public WorkPass, tenant-isolated organizations, SkillProof, and secure credentials.
- **Phase 7–8**: Jobs domain, flexible shifts domain, supervisor assignment, time slots.
- **Phase 9**: Application & Invitation transactional core, candidate key hardening (`V11`), employer pipeline, worker pipeline, and CI/CD automation.
- **Phase 10**: Workforce Pools with reusable worker membership, tenant isolation, duplicate protection, and integration tests.
- **Phase 11**: Recurring Availability & Overrides with IANA timezones, DST-aware resolution, date overrides, and isolation tests.
- **Phase 12**: Nearby Discovery already implemented through real PostGIS queries and GiST location indexes.
- **Phase 13**: Deterministic MatchEngine V1 with reproducible scoring, explainable breakdowns, and tenant-isolated match endpoints.
- **Phase 14**: Concurrency-safe shift reservations with row-level locking and high-contention capacity tests.
- **Phase 15**: Reusable Idempotency with request hashing, replayed responses, mismatch rejection, and concurrent retry tests.
- **Phase 16 (partial)**: New workforce operations and worker availability pages connect Phases 10–15 to real frontend workflows; remaining demo/static surfaces are still tracked separately.\n- **Phase 17**: Append-only WorkLedger with DB-enforced immutability and derived worker history projections.\n- **Phase 18**: Versioned TrustScore V1 with neutral cold start, bounded scoring, protected-trait-free inputs, and auditable snapshots.\n- **Phase 19**: Transactional Outbox with atomic commit/rollback evidence and retryable pending event storage.\n- **Phase 20**: Kafka transport and idempotent consumers with redelivery, ordering, consumer-crash retry, and poison-message tests.\n- **Phase 21**: In-app notifications and SSE with durable deduplication, reconnect cursor replay, unread state, and after-commit live delivery.\n- **Phase 22**: Redis optimization with read-through public detail caches, targeted invalidation, and outage-degradation tests.\n- **Phase 23**: OpenSearch public projections with full reindex, stale-data cleanup, and worker privacy projection tests.\n- **Phase 24**: Production malware scanning with full-object inspection, ClamAV integration, fail-closed quarantine, and scan telemetry tests.\n- **Phase 25**: OpenTelemetry tracing, request/trace correlation, structured JSON logs, Prometheus operational metrics, and redaction tests.
